Your PC is ready.

Windows is set up for gaming. All that's left is a few minutes of drivers.

Your next steps

  1. Chipset driver first, then restart

    AMD: pick Chipsets on the page. Intel: let the support tool detect your PC.

    AMD chipset Intel detect tool

    Why: it controls CPU power management and core scheduling. This matters most on X3D and dual-CCD CPUs.

  2. Graphics driver

    Install the one for your card. For NVIDIA, NVCleanstall gives you a lean install.

    NVIDIA AMD Radeon

    Optional, NVIDIA only: C:\ChamberTech\PostInstall\Drivers\NVIDIA\Nvidia Settings has Import .bat files. They apply ChamberTech's recommended NVIDIA settings.

  3. Network driver, only if your internet is missing or slow

    Not sure which you have? Try Intel first, then Realtek.

    Intel Realtek

That's it. Restart once more after the graphics driver and you're done.

Good to know

  • Secure Boot and TPM stay on. Anti-cheat like Vanguard, EAC and BattlEye needs them.
  • If Chrome or Brave was picked during setup, it's already installed. Microsoft Edge stays available either way.
  • This page won't open by itself again. The Driver Setup icon on your desktop brings it back.
  • Something not working? Contact ChamberTech.
